# Software Subscription, Auto-Renewal & License Policy
**Document ID:** SUB-POL-18 | **Version:** v2.0-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Subscription Services | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## Section 1.0 — Auto-Renewal Notification Rules
Subscribers must receive renewal notices 14 days prior to annual charge. Inadvertent renewals may be refunded within 14 days of charge.

## Section 2.0 — Prorated License Cancellation
Enterprise multi-seat licenses may be cancelled with prorated credit applied to future software purchases.
