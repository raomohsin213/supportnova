# Cross-Border Shipping, Customs Duties & Tariffs Policy
**Document ID:** INT-POL-16 | **Version:** v1.2-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Delivery & Logistics | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## Section 1.0 — Customs Clearance Delays
Delays due to customs inspection or import clearance are subject to an extended 14-day resolution window.

## Section 2.0 — Import Duty Dispute Resolution
Duties assessed by foreign customs authorities are the responsibility of the consignee unless DDP shipping was selected.
