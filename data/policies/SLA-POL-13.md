# Enterprise Service Level Agreements & Target Deadlines
**Document ID:** SLA-POL-13 | **Version:** v2.0-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Customer Support | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## Section 1.0 — Standard Response Timeframes
P1 Critical: 2 hours | P2 High: 8 hours | P3 Medium: 24 hours | P4 Low: 48 hours.

## Section 2.0 — SLA Breach Notification & 75% Risk Trigger
When elapsed ticket time reaches 75% of target SLA duration, an automated supervisor alert must be dispatched.
