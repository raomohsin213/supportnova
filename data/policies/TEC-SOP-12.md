## Section 1.0 — Firmware & Diagnostic Checklist
Support agents must walk customers through power-cycle, firmware verification, and reset sequences before issuing hardware RMA.

## Section 2.0 — Remote Diagnostic Assistance
Tier 2 Technical Support may request diagnostic logs or offer screen-share troubleshooting for enterprise appliances.
