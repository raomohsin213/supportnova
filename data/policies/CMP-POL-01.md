# Customer Complaint Intake & Triage Standard Policy
**Document ID:** CMP-POL-01 | **Version:** v1.2-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Customer Support | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## Section 1.0 — Intake Channels & Logging Requirements
All complaints submitted via Web, Email, Chat, or Uploaded Letter must be assigned a unique tracking ID within 60 seconds and logged into the central register.

## Section 2.0 — Severity Classification Protocols
Complaints are classified into P1 (Critical - 2h SLA), P2 (High - 8h SLA), P3 (Medium - 24h SLA), and P4 (Low - 48h SLA) based on objective business risk.
