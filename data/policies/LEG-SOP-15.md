# Legal Threat & Regulatory Agency Escalation SOP
**Document ID:** LEG-SOP-15 | **Version:** v1.0-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Legal & Privacy | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## Section 1.0 — Attorney and Litigation Isolation
Any correspondence containing attorney letterhead or threats of legal suit must halt normal triage and route to Corporate Legal Counsel.

## Section 2.0 — Strict Prohibition of Informal Settlements
Frontline agents are strictly forbidden from negotiating legal settlements or monetary payouts.
