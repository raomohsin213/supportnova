# Hazardous Equipment & Electrical Safety SOP
**Document ID:** SAF-SOP-05 | **Version:** v1.0-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Safety & Compliance | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## Section 1.0 — Immediate Cease-Use Directives
Any report mentioning smoke, spark, fire, chemical leakage, electrical shock, or physical injury mandates an immediate cease-use order sent to the customer.

## Section 2.0 — Emergency Response Escalation
All hazard reports must be assigned directly to Emergency Response / Product Safety with P1 Priority (2-hour target response) regardless of customer tone.
