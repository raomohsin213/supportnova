# Lost in Transit Investigation & Police Report SOP
**Document ID:** LST-SOP-17 | **Version:** v1.1-Active | **Status:** Active
**Category:** Delivery & Logistics | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15

---

## Section 1.0 — Porch Piracy & Theft Claims
Packages marked 'Delivered' but claimed missing require customer affidavit and filing of carrier theft inquiry.

## Section 2.0 — High-Value Stolen Goods Protocol
Packages valued over $250 require a local police report case number before secondary reshipment.
