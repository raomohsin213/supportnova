## Section 1.0 — CEO & Executive Office Escalation
Complaints addressed to company executives or board members must be transferred to the Special Inquiries Team within 1 hour.

## Section 2.0 — Executive Goodwill Discretion
Executive Escalation Managers possess discretion up to $500 for total issue resolution.
